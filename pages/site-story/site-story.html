01 / THE STARTA PERSONAL SPACE, NOT A LINK LIST

THE IDEA

One place for
the work and
the life around it.

I’m Noor, a designer and developer. I wanted the site to feel like a small portrait of me: what I make, what I explore, and what I enjoy away from work.

That meant bringing web and visual design, economics and statistics, piano, and photography into one experience. Each part needed room to be itself while still feeling like it belonged to the same person.

THE DESIGN INTENT

Bring my interests into one place while keeping each part clear, considered, and unmistakably mine.

HOW IT TOOK SHAPE

Four choices
made it personal.

The website grew by connecting its content, visual language, navigation, and small interactive details into one system.

01
START WITH WHAT’S REAL

Make room for the whole picture.

Design, research, piano, and photography each have their own space, so visitors can follow what interests them without losing the sense that it all belongs to one person.

✳
02
BUILD A VISUAL LANGUAGE

Keep the structure clear and the details expressive.

Deep green-black surfaces, warm paper tones, wine-red highlights, and teal accents give the pages a shared visual rhythm. Oversized serif headings bring character; compact labels keep the interface precise.

↗
03
MAKE SPACE TO EXPLORE

Let every interest have a clear path.

The side navigation stays familiar as the pages change. Sections, cards, and small labels help people understand where they are and where a link will take them.

⌂
04
ADD MOVEMENT WITH PURPOSE

Let small details bring the site to life.

Hover states, motion, the piano-note visualizer, and photography give the experience energy while keeping the content easy to read.

♫

A system with a point of view.

COLOR / TYPE / DETAIL
01 / COLOR

Dark, warm, and vivid.

Ink and soft charcoal make a quiet base. Paper, wine, and teal add contrast and identity.

02 / TYPE

Character meets clarity.

Roboto Slab gives headings their editorial weight. Barlow Condensed keeps body copy direct; IBM Plex Mono adds crisp labels and navigation.

ROBOTO SLABBarlow CondensedIBM Plex Mono
03 / DETAIL

One identity across pages.

Fine rules, offset color blocks, grain, geometric backdrops, and deliberate spacing make the system feel connected without flattening every page into the same layout.
