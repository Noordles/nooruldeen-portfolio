PLANNED STUDY

Start by making the comparisons fair.

PROJECT PLAN · DATA AND RESULTS NOT YET ADDED

Economic indicators can tell different stories when their definitions, population coverage, or time periods change. This project plan sets out how to check those choices before describing regional economic change.

01 / QUESTION

What changed, and for whom?

Compare a small number of indicators across selected economies and years, while keeping their denominators and definitions visible.

02 / EVIDENCE

Build a consistent panel

Potential measures include GDP per capita, employment status, labour-force participation, and unemployment. Each measure needs a source, unit, and coverage note.

03 / METHOD

Describe before explaining

Check missing years and revisions, then show levels and changes. A descriptive comparison alone cannot establish why an indicator moved.

Next steps

  • Choose the economies and years before looking at results.
  • Record indicator definitions and source vintages.
  • Check missingness and breaks in each series.
  • Publish the data and a short interpretation with the chart.

Possible data sources

World Development Indicators and ILOSTAT offer annual economic and labour series. The final project would document which indicators were selected and why.
