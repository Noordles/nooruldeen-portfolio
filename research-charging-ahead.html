HISTORICAL MARKET CLAIMS · 2023 CONTEXT

Charging Ahead is a student marketing study by Nooruldeen Al-Sammarraie, David Adrian Lițescu and Casandra Gagiu. It introduces Romanian electric-vehicle adoption, positions Tesla within the market, and develops recommendations for product, pricing, distribution, promotion and customer relationships.

From market scan to marketing mix

The presentation moves from an overview of the EV category to a company analysis and recommendations. Its strategy is proposed by the student team; the deck does not report a campaign test or measured business outcomes.

01 / PRODUCT

Improve & extend

Continue vehicle improvements and consider additional models and technology development.

02 / PRICE

Explain value

Balance premium positioning with total ownership costs, financing and potential incentives.

03 / PLACE

Access & service

Make vehicle discovery, charging access, delivery and after-sales service easier.

04 / PROMOTION

Build familiarity

Use digital content, customer stories and partnerships to reach distinct audiences.

What the team recommends

The strategy emphasizes technology and product development, a premium offer supported by a clearer cost-of-ownership story, expanded purchase and charging access, and digital promotion. For customer relationships, it highlights service quality, staff training and loyalty ideas.

The recommendations are proposals, not evidence that these actions were adopted or improved sales, satisfaction or retention.

How to use the market figures

The original deck presents figures from a 2023 market snapshot, including Romanian registrations and year-over-year changes. Because the deck is historical and does not provide a visible source list for every number, this page keeps the figures in their original time context rather than presenting them as current.

Use the embedded slides as the team’s original work and consult current industry sources before making present-day market decisions.

Scope note

This is a marketing coursework presentation. The SWOT and recommendations are the team’s analysis; there is no accompanying report or primary consumer research in the supplied files. Its market statistics are historical 2023 claims, and its strategy has not been evaluated in-market.
